Buying safely

Verify every offer before paying.

AI pricing changes often. The notes below are useful for discovery, but the official pricing page is the source to check before buying a plan or writing an affiliate recommendation.

Editorial rule: do not present a temporary discount as guaranteed. Mark trial periods, student deals, and annual-plan discounts clearly, and update this page when official pages change.
