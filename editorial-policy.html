Editorial Policy

Editorial rules for AI Tool Compass, including original explanations, beginner clarity, fact checking, affiliate disclosure, and avoiding scaled low-value content..

Helpful content standard

Every page should help a reader make a better decision or complete a task. Articles should include examples, practical steps, limits, and clear warnings where beginners commonly make mistakes.

Original value

We avoid scraped summaries, doorway pages, keyword stuffing, and thin affiliate pages. A guide should add original structure, plain-English explanation, comparison logic, and a useful workflow.

Corrections and updates

AI tools change quickly. When a product feature, price, policy, or capability changes, the affected article should be updated with a new modification date and clearer guidance.
