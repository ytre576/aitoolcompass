Review Methodology

How AI Tool Compass evaluates AI tools, writes comparisons, checks claims, handles updates, and separates editorial guidance from advertising. It is written for.

How guides are built

Each guide starts with a reader task, not a product pitch. We compare tools by workflow fit, ease of use, output quality, cost, limits, and the checks a beginner should run before trusting the result.

What we check

We look for clear use cases, realistic setup steps, common failure points, and whether a tool helps the reader finish a real job. Pricing and current product details should be verified against official pages before publication updates.

Advertising boundary

Advertising slots and affiliate links must not decide the editorial verdict. Sponsored placements should be labeled clearly and kept visually separate from recommendations.
